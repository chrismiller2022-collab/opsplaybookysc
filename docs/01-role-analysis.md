# Role analysis: how to be good at this job from day one

## What the job really is

You are the virtual front office for **several clubs at once**. Each has its own platform, contacts, season calendar and requests. Daily work covers phones, email, social media, registration and scheduling in the club's platform, sponsors, uniforms, travel, special requests and reporting. What gets you ahead is never dropping a thread across clients.

The posting's 30-day plan: onboard with a Sports Office Manager, shadow other admins, train on data analysis, CRM, registration platforms and workflow automation tools, then take on assigned clients alongside the manager.

## Before applying or reaching out

- **Portfolio piece:** a custom communication plan and operational calendar for a fictional club. Done: `03-riverbend-operations-playbook.md`.
- **Platform familiarity:** see `02-platform-learning-plan.md`. SportsEngine and GotSport are the most common.
- **Demo dashboard:** shows multi-client thinking in 30 seconds. Done: `04-command-center-dashboard.md`.

## The day-one system: a client command center

1. **Client profiles:** platform, key contacts and their preferred channel, season dates, sponsors, uniform vendor, refund policy, quirks. Keep logins in a password manager, never in a sheet.
2. **One request queue:** client, requester, channel, request, due date, status, time of first response. Every call, voicemail, email and DM becomes a row.
3. **Master deadline calendar:** all clients' operational calendars, color-coded, with reminders 7 days and 2 days out.
4. **Template library:** answers to the 50-times-a-week questions (refunds, how to register, schedule changes, uniform sizing, weather cancellations), per client, in English and Spanish.
5. **KPI tracker:** weekly per client. Response time, open requests, registrations vs last year, social engagement.

## Using AI so nothing slips

- **Morning brief:** deadlines in the next 7 days, requests over 24 hours with no reply, today's calls and meetings, grouped by client.
- **Inbox triage:** tag each message by client and urgency and draft a reply from templates. A human reviews and sends.
- **Calls and voicemails:** transcribe and extract action items into the queue.
- **Batched social media:** one weekly session drafts each client's posts from their calendar (game days, registration pushes, sponsor shoutouts).
- **Data analysis:** registration exports showing trends (an age group down 20%, a team near its cap).
- **End-of-week review:** compare the queue against the calendar and flag what's aging, overdue or at risk.

**Data caution:** this work involves youth athletes' and parents' personal data. Once hired, use the AI and CRM tools Sports Office approves. A good interview question: *"What's your current stack for CRM and workflow automation, and are you using AI anywhere yet?"*
