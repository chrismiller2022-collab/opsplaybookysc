## How to use this plan

You can get real hands-on time in three of the six platforms for free; the other three you learn from help centers, recorded trainings and the parent-side apps. Work them in this order: build a sandbox where it's free, take the course where one exists, and study the admin docs where neither is available.

- **Free sandbox:** TeamLinkt (fully free platform) and SportsEngine (free basic HQ account). Byga lists a free trial.
- **Free courses:** Sports Connect has an on-demand academy plus weekly live webinars.
- **Docs and recordings only:** GotSport and Demosphere have no public free tier, so use their support sites, state-association training recordings and the parent mobile apps.
- **Use the same fictional club everywhere.** Build Riverbend Youth Soccer Club (the portfolio club) in each sandbox so you can compare how each platform handles the identical job.
- **Be upfront in sales demos.** If you book a vendor demo, say you're a sports administrator learning the platform for client work. Don't pose as a club director; vendors talk to each other and to companies like Sports Office.

## The six platforms at a glance

Start with TeamLinkt and Sports Connect this week: both are free and cover the full registration-to-schedule workflow.

| Priority | Platform | Typical users | Free access | Best way to learn |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [TeamLinkt](https://teamlinkt.com/) | Rec leagues, community clubs, associations | Whole admin platform is free | Build a full fictional league yourself |
| 2 | [Sports Connect](https://learning.sportsconnect.com/all-courses/) (Stack Sports) | Rec leagues, AYSO-style programs, Little League-type orgs | Free on-demand academy courses | Take the New Admin Jumpstart and registration courses |
| 3 | [SportsEngine HQ](https://discover.sportsengineplay.com/solutions/hq-demo/) (NBC Sports) | Multi-sport clubs; volleyball, softball, hockey governing bodies | Free basic HQ account; free demo | Free account + help center videos |
| 4 | [GotSport](https://support.gotsport.com/) | Competitive and travel soccer, state associations, leagues | None public | Support articles + state association recordings |
| 5 | Byga | Competitive clubs (tryouts to evaluations) | Free trial listed | Trial, focused on tryouts and evaluations |
| 6 | [Demosphere](https://demosphere.com/) | Soccer clubs and state associations | None (paid, from about $600/yr) | Parent app + club help pages + YouTube |

## Platform by platform

Each platform gets the same five core reps: build a registration, set up teams and rosters, make a schedule, send a message, and pull a report.

### 1. TeamLinkt: your full sandbox

The league admin platform is free with no credit card, and its core tier includes registration forms, a schedule builder (with an AI schedule generator), team app, website, waivers, certifications, tournaments and reporting ([TeamLinkt](https://teamlinkt.com/), [FitGap summary](https://us.fitgap.com/products/056965/teamlinkt)).

- [ ] Create Riverbend YSC as an organization with U8, U10, U12 and U14 divisions
- [ ] Build a fall registration form: fees, sibling discount, waiver, birth-year eligibility
- [ ] Register 3 fake players (use your own email aliases) and place them on teams
- [ ] Generate a 6-week schedule for one division, then reschedule one game for weather
- [ ] Send a team message from the app and an org-wide announcement
- [ ] Export the registration report and note what columns you get

### 2. Sports Connect: take the courses

Stack Sports runs a free on-demand academy and weekly live training webinars for new admins; support is also reachable by phone at 866-258-3303 ([course list](https://learning.sportsconnect.com/all-courses/), [Sports Connect FAQ](https://sportsconnect.com/u-s-soccer-connect-user-forum-faq/)).

- [ ] New Admin Jumpstart
- [ ] Getting Started with Sports Connect (portal tour and admin dashboard)
- [ ] Building & Launching Registration
- [ ] Becoming a Lead Administrator (common mistakes when taking over a portal; very relevant to inheriting a client)
- [ ] Order Management Foundations (refunds and order changes, the top parent request)
- [ ] Getting Started with Stack Team App
- [ ] Sign up for one live training webinar and ask a question

### 3. SportsEngine HQ: free account plus help center

SportsEngine offers a free account tier, with premium tools for registration, league setup and background screening; it also offers free demos, an in-app help experience and a community forum ([NBC Sports announcement](https://nbcsportsgrouppressbox.com/2019/01/09/sportsengine-launches-sportsengine-hq-an-expanded-suite-of-solutions-for-youth-sports-organizations), [demo page](https://discover.sportsengineplay.com/solutions/hq-demo/)).

- [ ] Create a free SportsEngine account and explore what the free HQ tier allows
- [ ] In the help center, watch the videos on registration setup, rosters, invoices and the mobile app
- [ ] Learn how athlete profiles flow from registration into rosters, invoicing and cross-session reports
- [ ] Read how memberships and eligibility (background checks, SafeSport) are tracked
- [ ] Download the SportsEngine mobile app and look at it the way a parent would
- [ ] Optional: book a demo, introducing yourself honestly as an administrator learning the platform

### 4. GotSport: learn the admin tasks from guides

There is no public free tier, but state associations publish detailed club-admin guides and recorded training sessions ([GotSport support](https://support.gotsport.com/), [VYSA onboarding recordings](https://www.vysa.com/gotsport-onboarding/), [TCSL scheduling guide](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1IJ9z7gU0LT0acvbSZGrB90cs80JNFE50/export?format=pdf)).

- [ ] Watch the VYSA onboarding session recordings end to end
- [ ] From the TCSL guide, learn the five scheduling tasks: venues and pitches, club self-scheduling, rescheduling, admin schedule views, and exporting the club schedule for referee assignors
- [ ] Read the support articles on adding users to a club and updating coach or manager contacts
- [ ] Learn player cards and risk management (volunteer and coach background checks), since compliance is a big GotSport job
- [ ] Check which platform Georgia's state soccer association uses; local knowledge helps in an interview

### 5. Byga: tryout-to-evaluation workflow

Byga is built by coaches for competitive clubs and covers tryouts, registration and end-of-season player evaluations; review sites list a free trial ([GetApp](https://www.getapp.com/all-software/a/byga/)).

- [ ] Request the trial from Byga's site; if it requires a sales call, be upfront about why you want it
- [ ] Focus on what's unique: tryout registration, evaluations, attendance, and club-wide calendar
- [ ] Skip anything the other platforms already taught you

### 6. Demosphere: parent side plus club help pages

Demosphere has no free trial or version and starts around $600 a year, so learn it from the outside ([Software Advice](https://www.softwareadvice.com/za/cms/webwriter-profile/)). Its households register children, and only programs matching a player's birth year appear ([Coastal FC help](https://coastalfc.ca/registration-info)).

- [ ] Download the Demosphere mobile app and explore the parent and team views
- [ ] Read a few client clubs' Demosphere help pages (Coastal FC is a good example) to learn household accounts, RSVPs, calendar events and team reminders
- [ ] Watch Demosphere's YouTube partner and product videos
- [ ] Note the governing bodies that use it (Iowa Soccer and Kentucky Youth Soccer have announced partnerships)

## Two-week schedule

About 45 to 60 minutes a weekday gets you through all six platforms in two weeks.

| Day | Focus | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Week 1, Mon | TeamLinkt: org, divisions, registration form | Working fall registration |
| Week 1, Tue | TeamLinkt: players, teams, schedule, reschedule | 6-week schedule for one division |
| Week 1, Wed | TeamLinkt: messaging, reports | Exported registration report |
| Week 1, Thu | Sports Connect: Jumpstart + Getting Started | Notes on the admin dashboard |
| Week 1, Fri | Sports Connect: Registration + Lead Admin courses | List of common admin mistakes |
| Week 2, Mon | Sports Connect: Order Management + Team App | Refund and order-change steps |
| Week 2, Tue | SportsEngine: free account + help videos | Notes on registration to roster flow |
| Week 2, Wed | SportsEngine: eligibility, invoices, mobile app | Compliance tracking notes |
| Week 2, Thu | GotSport: VYSA recordings + TCSL scheduling guide | The five scheduling tasks, in your words |
| Week 2, Fri | Byga trial + Demosphere parent app | Comparison notes on all six |
