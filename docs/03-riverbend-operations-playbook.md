## Communication plan

Each channel has one job: email carries detail, the team app carries anything time-sensitive, and social media carries promotion and celebration.

| Moment | Audience | Channels | Timing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Registration opens | All families, past players, prospects | Email, social (3 posts over 2 weeks), website banner | Opening day, then weekly |
| Registration reminder | Past players not yet registered | Email, app push | 2 weeks and 72 hours before close |
| Team assignments | Registered families | Email with coach intro | 2 weeks before first practice |
| Season kickoff | Coaches, then families | Coach meeting; email; app | 1 week before first game |
| Schedule change | Affected teams only | App push and text | Same day, as soon as confirmed |
| Weather cancellation | Affected teams, referees | App push, social, website banner | At least 2 hours before kickoff |
| Game-day highlights | Community | Social | Weekend posts, Monday recap |
| Sponsor recognition | Community, sponsors | Social (tagged), website, banner photos | Monthly post per sponsor tier |
| Tryouts | Travel families, prospects | Email, social, website | Announce 6 weeks out; remind 1 week and 1 day out |
| End-of-season survey | All families | Email, app push | Within 3 days of last game |
| Sponsor renewal | Current sponsors | Personal email with season results | October |

Spanish versions go out for every family-facing email and post, matching the club's family mix.

## Response standards and escalation

Every call, email, voicemail and DM gets logged in one request queue and answered within one business day.

| Request type | First response | Resolution target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Game-day issue (field, referee, weather) | Within 1 hour | Same day |
| Registration or payment problem | Same business day | 2 business days |
| General question | 1 business day | 2 business days |
| Refund request | 1 business day | Per club refund policy, board approval if outside it |
| Complaint about a coach or player | Same business day (acknowledge only) | Escalate to director of coaching |

Escalation path:

1. Administrator resolves anything covered by club policy or a template.
2. Policy exceptions (refunds, late registrations, team moves) go to the club registrar or treasurer.
3. Coaching, conduct or safety concerns go to the director of coaching the same day.
4. Anything involving a child's safety goes to the club's designated safeguarding contact immediately and follows SafeSport reporting rules.

## KPI scorecard

Ten numbers, reviewed weekly with the club and monthly with the board, tell whether operations are healthy; sample values are illustrative.

| KPI | Definition | Target | Sample value |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Registrations vs last year | Paid registrations this season / same date last season | 100% or more | 104% |
| Player retention | Returning players / last season's eligible players | 80% | 76% |
| First-response time | Median hours from request logged to first reply | Under 4 business hours | 2.6 hours |
| Requests resolved on time | Share closed within the target in the response standards | 95% | 93% |
| Open requests over 2 days | Count of unresolved requests older than 2 business days | 0 | 3 |
| Coach clearance | Coaches with current background check and SafeSport before first practice | 100% | 100% |
| Outstanding balances | Unpaid fees / total fees billed | Under 5% | 6.8% |
| Sponsor renewal rate | Sponsors renewing / sponsors up for renewal | 85% | 83% (10 of 12) |
| Uniforms delivered on time | Teams with uniforms before first game | 100% | 97% |
| Family satisfaction | Average end-of-season survey score, 1 to 5 | 4.3 or more | 4.4 |

The sample shows how the scorecard drives action: retention and outstanding balances are below target, so the next step is a re-registration push to last season's non-returners and a payment-plan reminder to families with balances.

## First 30 days with a new client

The first month is about learning the club before changing anything, then delivering one visible quick win by day 30.

**Week 1: learn**

- [ ] Kickoff call: goals, pain points, decision-makers, preferred channels
- [ ] Get admin access to every platform, inbox, phone line and social account (logins stored in the password manager)
- [ ] Build the client profile: contacts, platform, season dates, sponsors, uniform vendor, refund policy
- [ ] Read the last 60 days of email to learn recurring questions

**Week 2: map**

- [ ] Fill in the club's operational calendar for the next 12 months
- [ ] Draft the top 15 reply templates, in English and Spanish
- [ ] Pull baseline numbers for the KPI scorecard

**Week 3: run**

- [ ] Take over the inbox and phones fully, with every request logged in the queue
- [ ] Send the first weekly status note to the club contact

**Week 4: improve**

- [ ] Deliver one quick win (for example, a registration FAQ that cuts repeat questions)
- [ ] 30-day review with the club: what's working, what to change, KPIs vs baseline
