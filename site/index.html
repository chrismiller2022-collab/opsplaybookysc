Sports Platform Learning Plan

A two-week, hands-on plan for SportsEngine, GotSport, Demosphere, Byga, Sports Connect and TeamLinkt.

The operating model

  1. Client profiles: one per club, covering platform, contacts, season dates, sponsors, vendors and policies.
  2. One request queue: every call, email, voicemail, DM and app message becomes a row.
  3. Master deadline calendar: every client's operational calendar merged, with reminders 7 and 2 days out.
  4. Template library: replies to recurring questions in English and Spanish.
  5. KPI tracker: weekly numbers per client, reviewed at check-ins.
All clubs, people and numbers on this site are fictional.
