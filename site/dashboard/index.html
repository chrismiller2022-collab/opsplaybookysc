CSV format

Pick up to four files named clients.csv, deadlines.csv, registrations.csv and requests.csv. Files are read in your browser and never leave it. Columns used:

  • clients: client, platform
  • deadlines: client, task, due (YYYY-MM-DD)
  • registrations: client, week_of_registration, this_season, last_season
  • requests: client, received (YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM, UTC), channel, category, status (Open or Resolved), hours_to_first_response, hours_to_resolve

The demo files (deadlines, registrations, requests) work as templates.
